About Elian

A practical approach to book visibility.

I work independently with authors who want more people to discover their books. My approach starts with the book itself: its genre, positioning, reader appeal and existing online presence.

From there, I identify practical opportunities and create or improve the visibility assets that support them. The goal is straightforward: give a good book more places and better reasons to be discovered.
